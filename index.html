About the forum

An annual gathering of Kazakhstan-connected scientists from universities and research institutions around the world, meeting professors, university leaders and industry from Kazakhstan in one room.

  1. 01

    Annual gathering of the QIST community

    Kazakhstan-connected scientists at international universities and research institutions, from assistant to full professors, meet in one place.

  2. 02

    Platform for international academic collaboration

    Researchers abroad meet professors and university leaders from Narxoz, SDU, KIMEP and other Kazakhstani institutions to start joint research and educational projects.

  3. 03

    Bridge between science and business

    Conversations about research commercialization, DeepTech startups, university entrepreneurship, technology transfer, and partnerships with companies and technoparks.

FAQ

Questions

Does registering guarantee a seat?

No. Registration is an application. Seats are limited, so the organizing team reviews every registration and selects participants. You can attend only if you receive a confirmation that you are invited.

How will I know if I am invited?

Confirmed participants receive an invitation by email before the forum. Please bring it with you. Without a confirmed invitation we cannot admit you on the day.

Do I need to be a QIST member?

No. Students, researchers, industry professionals and anyone interested in science can register. Invitations are sent after review.

Who is the forum for?

Kazakh scientists working abroad, professors and leaders of Kazakhstani universities, students and young researchers, founders of science-based startups, and companies looking for research partners.

Can I join online?

Some international speakers will contribute remotely. Details on online participation will be shared with registered participants.

Will I be able to talk to the speakers?

Yes. Besides talks on stage, the program has practical workshops and collaboration roundtables, so students and researchers can meet professors in small groups.

Who do I contact with questions?

Write to info@QISTA.org.
